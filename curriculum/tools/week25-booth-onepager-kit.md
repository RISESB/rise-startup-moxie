# Booth + One-Pager Kit (Week 25 tool)

Everything you need to work a table at the open house: one-pager copy, a booth setup checklist, and a 30-second and 2-minute version of your pitch.

In class this is a one-page AI tool students open in their own Claude account. RISE Coach can run the same task right in the chat: ask for the inputs below, then follow the task.

## Ask the student for
- Business name (e.g. Loop Keychains)
- Offer + price (e.g. custom keychains, $8 each)
- One line: what you do and for whom (e.g. custom team keychains for local youth sports families)
- 3 reasons someone should believe you - proof, not adjectives (e.g. sold 40 at the last tournament; handmade in one day; 3 teams already reorder)
- What do you want a visitor to DO at your booth? (e.g. scan the QR to preorder, or leave their number)

## The task
TASK: Build a booth kit for a student working a table at a public open house / showcase. Return exactly these four sections with '### ' headers, nothing before or after:

### One-pager copy
Write the ACTUAL words for a single printed page: a headline (bold, plain, not clever), a one-line subhead, 3 short bullets (turn their proof points into benefits a stranger gets), and a clear call-to-action line matching what they want visitors to do.

### 30-second pitch
Word-for-word, spoken, Problem-Solution-Ask. What they'd say to someone who stops for 20 seconds.

### 2-minute pitch
The fuller version for someone genuinely interested — story, what it is, proof, the ask. Still spoken, still tight.

### Booth setup checklist
A practical bring-and-do list for a table: what to display, a sign-up/QR capture so leads aren't lost, how to greet, and one thing most students forget. Keep it to bullets they can act on.

STUDENT INPUTS
- Name: [name]
- One-liner: [oneliner]
- Offer + price: [offer]
- Proof points: [proof]
- Desired visitor action: [ask]
