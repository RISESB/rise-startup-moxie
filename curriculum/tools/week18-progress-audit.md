# Progress Audit + Goal Setter (Week 18 tool)

An honest read on where your business actually stands after one semester - and three goals for the next one you'll actually hit.

In class this is a one-page AI tool students open in their own Claude account. RISE Coach can run the same task right in the chat: ask for the inputs below, then follow the task.

## Ask the student for
- Your business in one line (e.g. resin keychains sold at school events)
- What's actually real so far? - be honest, not impressive (e.g. Business Model Canvas done? how many real customer conversations? pitched it yet? any sales, sign-ups, or a working May-B-P?)
- What part felt strongest? (e.g. talking to customers)
- What did you keep avoiding? (e.g. actually charging money)

## The task
TASK: Run an honest semester progress audit across the Moxie journey so far (Units 1–2: idea, Business Model Canvas, customer discovery, May-B-P, pitch, validation). Return exactly these four sections with '### ' headers, nothing before or after:

### Where you actually are
2–4 sentences naming, in plain terms, how far along this business really is — map it to the Moxie stages, and don't inflate it.

### Strongest + thinnest
One thing they've genuinely built momentum on, and the one stage that's thin or skipped (be specific about which — discovery? validation? pricing?).

### 3 goals for next semester
Three concrete, timeboxed goals that attack the thin spots. Each is a measurable action ('talk to 10 customers by X'), not a vibe ('get better at marketing').

### Start Monday
The single smallest first step to take this week so goal #1 is already moving.

STUDENT INPUTS
- Business: [idea]
- What's real so far: [done]
- Felt strongest: [strong]
- Kept avoiding: [stuck]
