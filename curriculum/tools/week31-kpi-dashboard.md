# KPI Dashboard Builder (Week 31 tool)

The 3–5 numbers that actually tell you how your business is doing - what each one means, what "good" looks like, and one decision to make from them.

In class this is a one-page AI tool students open in their own Claude account. RISE Coach can run the same task right in the chat: ask for the inputs below, then follow the task.

## Ask the student for
- What does your business sell, and how? (e.g. keychains, sold in person at events and by DM order)
- What can you actually track right now? - sales, DMs, followers, foot traffic… (e.g. I know units sold, my cost per unit, Instagram followers, and roughly how many people ask vs. buy)
- Your latest real numbers - whatever you have, even rough (e.g. sold 32 units last month at $8, cost $3 each, ~120 people saw the table, 45 followers)

## The task
TASK: Help this student build a tiny KPI dashboard and actually read it. Pick KPIs that fit THEIR business — don't dump a generic list. Return exactly these four sections with '### ' headers, nothing before or after:

### Your KPIs
3–5 key numbers worth tracking, each as: **the KPI** — one plain-English line on what it measures and why it matters for their specific business. Where their numbers let you, compute it (e.g. conversion rate = buyers ÷ people who saw it; margin = price − cost).

### What good looks like
For 2–3 of those KPIs, a rough sense of a healthy range or direction, so they can tell a good number from a bad one. Be honest that these are rules of thumb, not laws.

### The one number to watch now
Given what they told you, the single KPI they should watch most closely right now, and why it's the bottleneck.

### One decision from the data
A specific, concrete decision or test their numbers point to this week (change price? drive more foot traffic? fix the ask?). Tie it to the number.

If key numbers are missing, name exactly which one they should start tracking and how — don't invent values.

STUDENT INPUTS
- Business: [biz]
- Can track: [measure]
- Latest numbers: [numbers]
