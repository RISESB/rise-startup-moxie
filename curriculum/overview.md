A full year of building real businesses, one week at a time.

- Week 1: What Is Entrepreneurship? Mindset, Practice, and Life Design
- Week 2: Finding Your Why: Values, Strengths, and Life Design
- Week 3: Observing Your World: Walk, Talk, Show Up

- Week 19: Customer Segmentation + Messaging

- Week 32: Team Roles + Operational Planning
- Week 33: End of Year Recap

## Unit 5: The Showcase (Capstone)
Packaging the year into a brand snapshot and presenting it live at the student showcase.

- Week 34: The End Part 1: Brand + Business Snapshot
