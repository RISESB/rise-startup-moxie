# Week 21: Digital Marketing Tools

Unit 3: Marketing & Brand

**Guiding question:** What are the actual tools you use to put your brand in front of your customer — and how do you use them well?

_When it last ran: Week of January 26, 2026 · 7:30–9:00 AM · Mon–Fri. Dates are from a previous year; this year's calendar may differ._

## By the end of the week, students can
- Identify the 2-3 digital channels that best fit their segment.
- Build one real marketing asset (post, landing page, or short video).
- Understand the basics of organic vs. paid reach.
- Set up a simple way to capture interested leads.

## Lesson walkthrough
_Written as the facilitator's script for the classroom. Use it to know what was taught and how; don't read it back to students._

### Monday

### Monday — Lecture: The Digital Marketing Toolkit

Facilitator Goal: Students understand the main digital marketing tools and choose the few that fit their customer — instead of trying to use all of them.

#### 7:30–7:35 AM — Warm-Up

"Where did you last discover a product or business online? What platform, what made you stop scrolling?" 3-4 students share.

#### 7:35–8:05 AM — Lecture: Digital Marketing Tools

Opening (2 min)

Say: "There are dozens of marketing tools. You do not need all of them. You need the 2-3 where your specific segment already spends time. Remember Week 19 — go where they are."

The Main Channels (10 min)

Social media (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn) — best for awareness and story

Landing pages (Carrd, Google Sites) — one page that turns interest into a signup or sale

Short video (Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts) — highest reach for most young founders right now

Email/text lists — the only audience you actually own; great for repeat customers

Local + community (flyers, events, community groups) — still powerful for local businesses

"Match the channel to the segment. Selling to teens? Short video. Selling to local parents? Facebook groups and community events. Selling to businesses? LinkedIn and direct email."

Organic vs. Paid (6 min)

Organic = free reach you earn with content. Slow but builds trust. Paid = you pay to put your message in front of people. Fast but stops when the money stops.

"Start organic. Prove the message works for free before you ever pay to boost it. Paying to promote a message that does not work just loses money faster."

Capturing Leads (6 min)

Say: "The biggest mistake: getting attention and having nowhere to send it. Every marketing asset needs a next step — a link, a signup, a DM to send, a form to fill."

"A simple Google Form or a link in your bio is enough to start. Capture interested people so you can follow up."

#### 8:05–8:50 AM — Channel Selection + Asset Plan

Students pick their top 2-3 channels (matched to their segment) and decide the ONE marketing asset they will build Tuesday: a post, a landing page, or a short video.

Walk the room: "Does your channel match where your segment actually is? What is the next step in your asset — what do you want people to do?"

#### 8:50–9:00 AM — Close

Preview: "Tomorrow you build a real asset you can actually publish."

### Tuesday

### Tuesday — Activities: Build a Real Marketing Asset

#### 7:30–7:35 AM — Warm-Up

"What asset are you building today, and who is it for?" Around the room fast.

#### 7:35–8:45 AM — Build Time

Students build one real, publishable asset. It must include their message (Week 19), reflect their brand (Week 20), and have a clear next step.

AI support: "Draft 3 captions for an Instagram post advertising [my business] to [my segment]." Or: "Write the copy for a one-page landing page for [my business]." Then edit until it sounds like them.

Build tracks:

Social post: image/graphic in Canva + caption + call to action

Landing page: headline, problem, solution, one clear button (Carrd or Google Sites)

Short video: 15-30 second script + film it on a phone

Walk the room: "What is the next step for someone who sees this? Is the link/form ready?"

Goal: every student leaves with something they could publish today.

#### 8:45–9:00 AM — Speaker Tour Prep

Distribute Speaker Tour Prep Worksheets.

### Friday

### Friday — Profit or Broke + 1-on-1s + Week Reflection

#### 7:30–7:35 AM — Warm-Up

"Did you publish your asset? What happened — any views, clicks, or replies?" Around the room.

#### 7:35–8:05 AM — Profit or Broke (Week 21 — Rotation: Profit or Broke)

Marketing-cost edition:

Scenario 1: "You spend $30 boosting a post. It reaches 2,000 people, 15 click your link, 2 buy at $25. Profit or Broke?"

Answer: Revenue $50 - $30 = $20 profit. Follow up: "What is a click worth to you? A follower?"

Scenario 2: "You spend 6 hours making free content. 1 post goes semi-viral, 400 people visit your form, 8 sign up, 3 buy at $40. Profit or Broke?"

Answer: $120 revenue, $0 cash spent (but time). Follow up: "How do you value your time here?"

Scenario 3: "You pay $100 for a designer, $50 for ads. You make 4 sales at $35. Profit or Broke?"

Answer: $140 - $150 = -$10. BROKE. Follow up: "What would you change before spending on design again?"

#### 8:05–8:40 AM — 1-on-1 Sessions

1-on-1 focus: "What is your one channel to go deep on this month? What is the next asset?"

#### 8:40–8:55 AM — Week Reflection

Which channel fits your segment best and why?

What did you publish, and what is the next step for someone who sees it?

What is one thing you learned about marketing tools this week?
