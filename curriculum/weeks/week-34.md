# Week 34: The End Part 1: Brand + Business Snapshot

Unit 5: The Showcase (Capstone)

**Guiding question:** How do you package everything you built this year into one clear, polished picture of your business?

## By the end of the week, students can
- Finalize their brand look and messaging for public presentation.
- Build a one-page Business Snapshot that captures the whole business.
- Pull their strongest year-long evidence into a clean showcase deck.
- Get feedback and polish before rehearsal week.

## The week at a glance
- **Monday:** Kickoff — What a Showcase-Ready Business Looks Like
- **Tuesday:** Build the Business Snapshot + Brand Polish
- **Wednesday:** Speaker/Tour
- **Thursday:** Build the Showcase Deck + Peer Feedback
- **Friday:** Founder Fight + Polish + Week Reflection

## Key ideas (from the slides)
- **Warm-Up: The Finish Line:** In 3 weeks you present · What should they walk away thinking?
- **What "Showcase-Ready" Means:** A stranger gets your business in 2 minutes — and believes it's real
- **Three Pieces Get You There:** Business Snapshot · Showcase Deck · Confident presentation
- **The Business Snapshot:** One page · the whole business at a glance
- **What Goes On It:** Name + one-line · Problem + who has it · Solution + USP · Traction · Your ask
- **Traction Is Proof:** Real numbers · sales · customers · tests
- **Curation, Not Creation:** It all already exists — pull it from your portfolio
- **Activity: Snapshot First Draft:** Draft your one page · Could a stranger get it in 60 seconds?
- **The Stranger Test:** What's missing? · What's fuzzy?
- **Close:** Next: finalize the snapshot & polish your brand look

## Lesson walkthrough
_Written as the facilitator's script for the classroom. Use it to know what was taught and how; don't read it back to students._

### Monday

### Monday — Kickoff: What a Showcase-Ready Business Looks Like

Facilitator Goal: Students understand exactly what they are building over the next three weeks and start assembling their snapshot.

#### 7:30–7:40 AM — Warm-Up: The Finish Line

"In three weeks you present your business to a room of family, community members, and guests. In one sentence — what do you want them to walk away thinking about you?" Around the room.

#### 7:40–8:05 AM — What Showcase-Ready Means

Say: "Showcase-ready means someone who has never met you understands your business in 2 minutes and believes it is real. Three pieces get you there:"

A Business Snapshot — one page that captures the whole business at a glance

A Showcase Deck — a clean, visual walk-through of your story and results

A confident spoken presentation — which we rehearse next week

The Business Snapshot — one page containing:

Business name + one-line description

The problem + who has it

Your solution + what makes it different (USP)

Traction — real numbers, sales, customers, or tests

Your ask / what is next

#### 8:05–8:50 AM — Snapshot First Draft

Students draft their one-page Business Snapshot, pulling from their portfolio. Everything already exists — this is curation, not creation.

Walk the room: "Could a stranger read this and get your business in 60 seconds? What is missing or fuzzy?"

#### 8:50–9:00 AM — Close

Preview: "Tomorrow you finalize the snapshot and polish your brand look."

### Tuesday

### Tuesday — Build the Business Snapshot + Brand Polish

#### 7:30–7:35 AM — Warm-Up

"Read your one-line business description. Room says: clear or fuzzy." Fast round.

#### 7:35–8:15 AM — Finalize the Snapshot

Students finish their Business Snapshot in a clean format (Canva, Docs, or Slides). It should look professional and be printable for the showcase table.

AI support: "Here is my business snapshot text. Tighten it so a stranger understands my business in 60 seconds, and flag anything vague."

#### 8:15–8:45 AM — Brand Polish

Students finalize their brand for public presentation: consistent name, colors, logo/wordmark, and voice across the snapshot and deck. Reuse Week 20 brand work.

"Consistency is what makes a small business look legit. Same look everywhere."

#### 8:45–9:00 AM — Speaker Tour Prep

Distribute Speaker Tour Prep Worksheets.

### Wednesday

### Wednesday — Speaker/Tour

Run the speaker or tour per standard Wednesday format. If possible, invite a past program alum or the kind of guest who might attend the showcase. Prompt: "Ask the speaker what makes a business presentation memorable."

### Thursday

### Thursday — Build the Showcase Deck + Peer Feedback

#### 7:30–7:35 AM — Warm-Up

"One sentence — most interesting thing from Wednesday." Around the room.

#### 7:35–7:50 AM — Thank You Cards

Quick, specific, handwritten thank you cards for Wednesday's speaker.

#### 7:50–8:35 AM — Build the Showcase Deck

Students build a clean showcase deck (evolve the Week 17 McCloskey deck with a full year of results). Suggested flow: Cover → Problem → Solution → Market → Traction/Results → What I Learned → What's Next.

"This is the best version of the deck you have built all year. More proof, more polish, more you.");

#### 8:35–9:00 AM — Peer Feedback

Pairs review each other's snapshot + deck. Feedback: one thing that is strong, one thing that is unclear, one question a showcase guest would ask.

### Friday

### Friday — Founder Fight + Polish + Week Reflection

#### 7:30–7:35 AM — Warm-Up

"One thing you fixed based on yesterday's feedback." Around the room.

#### 7:35–8:05 AM — Founder Fight (Week 34 — Rotation: Founder Fight)

Showcase-readiness edition:

Round 1: A gorgeous deck with weak results vs. a plain deck with strong real results

Round 2: A founder who tells a story vs. one who lists facts

Round 3: A snapshot a stranger gets in 30 seconds vs. one that needs explaining

Debrief: "What actually makes a business presentation land with a real audience?"

#### 8:05–8:40 AM — Polish + 1-on-1s

Students polish snapshot and deck. 1-on-1 focus: "Is your snapshot showcase-ready? What is the one weak slide to fix before rehearsal week?"

#### 8:40–8:55 AM — Week Reflection

What is the one-line version of your business for the showcase?

What is your strongest piece of proof?

What are you most proud to show?

#### 8:55–9:00 AM — Preview Week 35

Week 35: The End Part 2 — Showcase Prep. "Next week we rehearse until you can present with confidence, and get every detail ready for the big day."
