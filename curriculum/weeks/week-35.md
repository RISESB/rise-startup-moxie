# Week 35: The End Part 2: Showcase Prep

Unit 5: The Showcase (Capstone)

**Guiding question:** Can you present your business with confidence, handle real questions, and make your booth impossible to walk past?

## By the end of the week, students can
- Deliver a polished showcase presentation from memory.
- Handle audience questions with confidence.
- Set up a professional showcase booth/display.
- Run a full dress rehearsal and fix the last gaps.

## The week at a glance
- **Monday:** Presentation Skills + First Run-Throughs
- **Tuesday:** Q&A Practice + Booth Setup Plan
- **Wednesday:** Speaker/Tour
- **Thursday:** Full Dress Rehearsal
- **Friday:** Deal or No Deal + Final Prep + Confidence Close

## Lesson walkthrough
_Written as the facilitator's script for the classroom. Use it to know what was taught and how; don't read it back to students._

### Monday

### Monday — Presentation Skills + First Run-Throughs

Facilitator Goal: Students deliver a first full run-through and learn the presentation basics that make them look and feel confident.

#### 7:30–7:35 AM — Warm-Up

"What scares you most about presenting at the showcase?" Around the room. Name it — it shrinks when spoken.

#### 7:35–7:55 AM — Presentation Skills Mini-Lesson

Quick, practical coaching:

Strong open — first 15 seconds hook them (a stat, a question, a story), not "um, so, my business is...")

Eyes up — talk to people, not slides. Know your deck so you don't read it.

Slow down — nerves speed you up. Pause. Breathe. Let points land.

Body — stand tall, hands open, feet planted.

Strong close — end with your ask and one memorable line, not "yeah, that's it."

#### 7:55–8:50 AM — First Run-Throughs

Small groups of 4-5. Every student delivers their full presentation once. Group gives feedback: one strength, one fix.

Facilitator rotates, coaching the specific skills above. Watch for reading off slides and rushing.

#### 8:50–9:00 AM — Close

Homework: "Practice your presentation out loud 3 times before tomorrow. Time it.");

### Tuesday

### Tuesday — Q&A Practice + Booth Setup Plan

#### 7:30–7:35 AM — Warm-Up

"Deliver your first 15 seconds — your hook — to the person next to you." Everyone at once, then 3-4 to the room.

#### 7:35–8:10 AM — Q&A Gauntlet

Students present in small groups; the group fires real questions. Practice handling tough ones calmly.

Common showcase questions to prepare for:

"How much money have you actually made?"

"Who is your customer, exactly?"

"What was the hardest part?"

"Is this a real business or a school project?"

"What is next after this year?"

Coach: "It is okay to say 'I don't know yet, but here's how I'd find out.' Honesty beats bluffing."

#### 8:10–8:45 AM — Booth Setup Plan

Students plan their physical showcase booth: snapshot printed, product/demo, visual/sign, signup or contact method, and how they will greet visitors. Reuse Week 25 Open House skills.

#### 8:45–9:00 AM — Speaker Tour Prep

Distribute Speaker Tour Prep Worksheets.

### Wednesday

### Wednesday — Speaker/Tour

Run the speaker or tour per standard Wednesday format. Keep it lighter this week if students need the time for rehearsal prep.

### Thursday

### Thursday — Full Dress Rehearsal

Facilitator Goal: Run the showcase exactly as it will happen next week, so the real thing feels like a repeat.

#### 7:30–7:40 AM — Warm-Up + Setup

Students set up their booths exactly as planned. Treat it as the real event.

#### 7:40–8:40 AM — Dress Rehearsal

Run the full showcase format end to end. Half present while half play guests (and invite staff or other classes to walk through if possible). Then switch.

Facilitator + guest reviewers give each student one specific, kind, useful note.

#### 8:40–9:00 AM — Fix List + Thank You Cards

Each student writes their top 2 fixes before the showcase. Quick thank you cards for any guest reviewers or Wednesday's speaker.

### Friday

### Friday — Deal or No Deal + Final Prep + Confidence Close

#### 7:30–7:35 AM — Warm-Up

"One fix you made since the dress rehearsal." Around the room.

#### 7:35–8:00 AM — Deal or No Deal (Week 35 — Rotation: Deal or No Deal)

Keep it fun and confidence-building this week. Fast scenarios, high energy — shake off nerves before the big day.

#### 8:00–8:40 AM — Final Prep + 1-on-1s

Students make final fixes to deck, snapshot, and booth materials. 1-on-1 focus: "Are you ready? What is the one thing you still need to do before the showcase?"

#### 8:40–9:00 AM — Confidence Close

Go around the room: each student says one sentence — "At the showcase, I want people to see ___." End on high energy.

Preview Week 36: "Next week is the Student Showcase. You have done the work all year. Now you show the world. Bring everything — and bring your confidence."
