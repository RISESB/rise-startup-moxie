# Week 33: End of Year Recap

Unit 4: Finance & Operations

**Guiding question:** How far have you actually come this year — and how do you tell that story?

_When it last ran: Week of April 20, 2026 · 7:30–9:00 AM · Mon–Fri. Dates are from a previous year; this year's calendar may differ._

## Lesson walkthrough
_Written as the facilitator's script for the classroom. Use it to know what was taught and how; don't read it back to students._

### Monday

### Monday — Year in Review + Growth Reflection

Facilitator Goal: Students see how far they have come — in their business and as people — and feel it.

#### 7:30–7:40 AM — Warm-Up: Week 1 vs. Now

"Think back to Week 1. What did you think entrepreneurship was then? What do you know now that you didn't?" 4-5 students share.

If you kept their Week 1 Entrepreneurial Identity sheets, hand them back now. The reactions are the whole point.

#### 7:40–8:15 AM — Year in Review Walk-Through

Walk the class through the arc of the year, unit by unit. For each, students jot the biggest thing they built or learned:

Unit 1 (Weeks 1-6): mindset, your why, observing problems, first pitch

Unit 2 (Weeks 7-17): design thinking, customer discovery, MVP, sales, the final pitch

Unit 3 (Weeks 18-30): marketing, campaigns, and the finances

Unit 4 (Weeks 31-33): analytics, operations, and now — the recap

#### 8:15–8:50 AM — Growth Reflection

Students write honestly about two kinds of growth:

Business growth — what is real now that did not exist in September (customers, sales, product, skills)

Personal growth — how they think, speak, or show up differently than they did in Week 1

"The business might be big or small. That is not the point. The point is who you became by building it."

#### 8:50–9:00 AM — Close

Preview: "Tomorrow you gather the proof — the artifacts and numbers that tell your story."

### Tuesday

### Tuesday — Evidence Gathering + Story Draft

#### 7:30–7:35 AM — Warm-Up

"What is the single best piece of proof that your business is real?" Around the room.

#### 7:35–8:10 AM — Evidence Gathering

Students pull their strongest artifacts from the whole year into one place for the showcase: pitch deck, best campaign, sales/traction numbers, customer quotes, photos of product or events, dashboard.

"You have been building this portfolio all year. Now you curate the highlights."

#### 8:10–8:45 AM — Year-End Story Draft

Students draft their year-end story using a simple arc: where I started → what I built → what I learned → where I am headed.

"This becomes the backbone of your showcase presentation in a few weeks. Get the honest version down now."

#### 8:45–9:00 AM — Speaker Tour Prep

Distribute Speaker Tour Prep Worksheets.

### Wednesday

### Wednesday — Speaker/Tour

Run the speaker or tour per standard Wednesday format. Prompt: "Ask the speaker what they wish they had known at the start of their journey."

### Thursday

### Thursday — Speaker Recap + Thank You Cards + Portfolio Cleanup

#### 7:30–7:35 AM — Warm-Up

"One sentence — most interesting thing from Wednesday." Around the room.

#### 7:35–7:55 AM — Speaker Recap

Two columns: "What we heard" | "What part of my year does this connect to?"

#### 7:55–8:20 AM — Thank You Cards

Handwritten. Specific. Required elements: one specific observation, one question it sparked, a genuine thank you.

#### 8:20–9:00 AM — Portfolio Cleanup

Students organize their portfolio into a clean, presentable state for the showcase build. 1-on-1 focus: "What is your strongest evidence, and what is the one-line version of your year?"

### Friday

### Friday — Profit or Broke + 1-on-1s + Showcase Preview

#### 7:30–7:35 AM — Warm-Up

"One number that shows your progress this year." Around the room.

#### 7:35–8:00 AM — Profit or Broke (Week 33 — Rotation: Profit or Broke)

Year-in-review edition: use real numbers from students' actual businesses this year. Celebrate the wins and treat the losses as lessons.

#### 8:00–8:35 AM — 1-on-1 Sessions

1-on-1 focus: "What are you most proud of this year? What is the story you want to tell at the showcase?"

#### 8:35–8:55 AM — Showcase Preview

Introduce the 3-week finale: Week 34 you build your brand + business snapshot, Week 35 you prep and rehearse, Week 36 is the Student Showcase — your businesses presented to family, community, and guests.

"This is the finish line you have been running toward all year. Time to make it count."

#### 8:55–9:00 AM — Week Reflection

One sentence each: "The person I was in Week 1 vs. the person I am now."
