# Week 13: Choosing the Right Channels + Competitive Research + SWOT Analysis

Unit 2: Customer & Product

**Guiding question:** How do you reach your customer where they already are — and what makes you different from everyone else trying to reach them too?

_When it last ran: Week of November 17, 2025 · 7:30–9:00 AM · Mon–Fri. Dates are from a previous year; this year's calendar may differ._

## By the end of the week, students can
- Identify the 3 most effective channels for reaching their specific customer.
- Conduct basic competitive research on 2-3 competitors.
- Complete a SWOT analysis for their business.
- Use competitive insights to sharpen their value proposition.

## Lesson walkthrough
_Written as the facilitator's script for the classroom. Use it to know what was taught and how; don't read it back to students._

### Monday

### Monday — Lecture: Distribution Channels + Competitive Landscape

Facilitator Goal: Students can name and explain their top 3 distribution channels and explain what makes a channel right for their specific customer.

#### 7:30–7:35 AM — Warm-Up

"How did you find out about the last thing you bought? Walk me through it." 4-5 students share. Write the channels on the board.

#### 7:35–8:05 AM — Lecture: Channels + Competition

Opening (2 min)

Say: "You can build a great product, price it perfectly, and target the right customer — and still fail if you are trying to reach them in the wrong place. The channel is how you reach your customer. The right channel is where they already are, already trust information, and are already making decisions."

Types of Channels (8 min)

Direct channels:

In person: events, markets, door-to-door, community spaces

Direct outreach: DMs, texts, calls, emails

Your own social media: your audience

Indirect channels:

Referrals: existing customers tell others

Partnerships: another business sends you customers

Marketplaces: Etsy, eBay, Facebook Marketplace

Press and content: stories, podcasts, blogs

Say: "The best channel is the one your specific customer already uses and trusts. Go back to your persona — where does your customer spend time?"

"The worst approach: try every channel and be mediocre everywhere. Pick one or two and be excellent there first."

Competitive Research (8 min)

Say: "You are never the only solution. Even if there is no direct competitor, your customer has a workaround — and that workaround is your real competition."

How to research:

Google the problem from your customer's perspective — what comes up?

Search your customer's watering holes — what do people recommend?

Look at review sites, Reddit, local Facebook groups — who are people already paying?

AI prompt: "What are the existing solutions for [your problem]? What do customers complain about most?"

#### 8:05–8:50 AM — Channel Mapping

Students list all possible channels, score each on (1) how well it reaches their persona and (2) feasibility right now. Pick top 3.

Walk the room: "Your persona spends time at [place from their persona]. Is that channel on your list?"

#### 8:50–9:00 AM — Close

Preview Tuesday: "Tomorrow you research your competition and build your SWOT."

### Tuesday

### Tuesday — Activities: Competitive Research + SWOT Analysis

#### 7:30–7:35 AM — Warm-Up

"Name one competitor — direct or indirect — and what they do better than you right now." Around the room.

#### 7:35–8:10 AM — Competitive Research

Research 2-3 competitors. For each: name, offer and price, target customer, what they do well (positive reviews), what they do badly (negative reviews), how your business compares.

#### 8:10–8:50 AM — SWOT Analysis

Strengths and Weaknesses = internal (your business now). Opportunities and Threats = external (market, competition, environment).

Strengths: what do you have that competitors don't? Your Bird-in-Hand is a clue.

Weaknesses: what do your discovery interviews say you still don't have?

Opportunities: what gap in the competitive landscape did you find?

Threats: what could kill this — market shift, a competitor copying you, cost changes?

After: "Circle your biggest Strength and biggest Opportunity. That intersection is your competitive advantage."

#### 8:50–9:00 AM — Speaker Tour Prep

Distribute Speaker Tour Prep Worksheets.

### Wednesday

### Wednesday — Speaker/Tour

Run the speaker or tour per standard Wednesday format.

### Thursday

### Thursday — Speaker Recap + Thank You Cards + SWOT + Channel Planning

#### 7:30–7:35 AM — Warm-Up

"One sentence — most interesting thing from Wednesday." Around the room.

#### 7:35–7:55 AM — Speaker Recap

Two columns: "What channels did this person use?" | "What was their competitive advantage?"

#### 7:55–8:20 AM — Thank You Cards

Handwritten. Specific. Required elements: one specific observation, one question it sparked, a genuine thank you.

#### 8:20–9:00 AM — Channel Plan + SWOT Refinement

Students finalize channel plan (top 3 with one action each) and refine SWOT. 1-on-1 focus: "What is your competitive advantage — the specific thing that makes a customer choose you over their current workaround?"

### Friday

### Friday — Founder Fight + 1-on-1s + Week Reflection

#### 7:30–7:35 AM — Warm-Up

"One sentence: your competitive advantage." Around the room. Note which are specific vs. vague.

#### 7:35–8:05 AM — Founder Fight (Week 13 — Rotation: Founder Fight)

Round 1: A business that sells only through social media vs. one that sells only in person

Round 2: A business with no competitors vs. one with 10 established competitors

Round 3: A business with one strong channel vs. one that tries to be everywhere

Debrief: "What makes a channel strategy strong vs. scattered? What does having competition actually signal about a market?"

#### 8:05–8:40 AM — 1-on-1 Sessions

1-on-1 focus: "Walk me through your SWOT. What opportunity excites you most? What threat worries you most?"

#### 8:40–8:55 AM — Week Reflection

What did you learn about your competition that changed how you see your business?

What is your most important channel and one action you will take in it this week?

What is your one clearest competitive advantage right now?

#### 8:55–9:00 AM — Preview Week 14

Week 14: Sales Strategy — Processes, People, and Funnels. "Next week we build an actual sales process."
