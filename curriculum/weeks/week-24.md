# Week 24: Launch a Real Marketing Campaign

Unit 3: Marketing & Brand

**Guiding question:** Can you run a real campaign, in the real world, and produce real results this week?

_When it last ran: Week of February 16, 2026 · 7:30–9:00 AM · Presidents Day Monday — lecture shifts to Tuesday if off · Presidents Day Monday — lecture shifts to Tuesday if off. Dates are from a previous year; this year's calendar may differ._

## By the end of the week, students can
- Launch a live marketing campaign to their real segment.
- Track results with real metrics (reach, clicks, leads, sales).
- Respond and adjust mid-campaign based on what they see.
- Report what worked, what didn't, and what they'd do next.

## Lesson walkthrough
_Written as the facilitator's script for the classroom. Use it to know what was taught and how; don't read it back to students._

### Monday

### Monday — Presidents Day / Campaign Prep

If Presidents Day is observed: NO CLASS. Launch prep moves to Tuesday.

If school is in session: use today for final campaign prep — confirm all pieces, schedule posts, set up tracking — and go live Tuesday.

### Tuesday

### Tuesday — Launch Prep + Go Live

Facilitator Goal: Every student gets a real campaign live and a tracking sheet running.

#### 7:30–7:35 AM — Warm-Up

"What is your campaign goal, and what number would make this week a win?" Around the room. Write goals on the board.

#### 7:35–8:00 AM — Final Launch Check

Students run a pre-launch checklist:

All pieces point to ONE next step (link, form, DM)

Lead capture is set up and tested (does the form actually work?)

A/B versions are ready to post at the planned times

Tracking sheet is built: date, piece, reach, clicks, leads, sales

#### 8:00–8:45 AM — GO LIVE

Students publish the first pieces of their campaign in class. Post, send, hand out, or share — whatever their channel is. Then log the launch in their tracking sheet.

Walk the room: "It's live? Good. What is your version A vs. version B, and when does B go out?"

For students whose channel is offline (flyers, events): confirm distribution plan and first drop today.

#### 8:45–9:00 AM — Speaker Tour Prep

Distribute Speaker Tour Prep Worksheets.

### Wednesday

### Wednesday — Speaker/Tour

Run the speaker or tour per standard Wednesday format. Prompt: "Ask the speaker how they got their first 10 customers."

### Thursday

### Thursday — Speaker Recap + Thank You Cards + Mid-Campaign Adjust

#### 7:30–7:35 AM — Warm-Up

"One number from your campaign so far — reach, clicks, leads, or sales." Around the room.

#### 7:35–7:55 AM — Speaker Recap

Two columns: "What we heard" | "What could I apply to my live campaign today?"

#### 7:55–8:15 AM — Thank You Cards

Handwritten. Specific. Required elements: one specific observation, one question it sparked, a genuine thank you.

#### 8:15–9:00 AM — Mid-Campaign Adjust

Students review live data and make one real adjustment: post the winning A/B version more, change a weak hook, follow up with everyone who clicked, or push the offer harder.

Say: "Real marketers don't set it and forget it. They watch and adjust. What is your data telling you to do right now?"

1-on-1 focus: "What is working? What is dead? What is your one adjustment before Friday?"

### Friday

### Friday — Profit or Broke + Results Report + Week Reflection

#### 7:30–7:35 AM — Warm-Up

"Final campaign number — your best result of the week." Around the room.

#### 7:35–8:00 AM — Profit or Broke (Week 24 — Rotation: Profit or Broke)

Use real campaign numbers from students this week. "You spent $X and time. You got Y leads and Z sales. Profit or Broke — and what is a lead worth?"

#### 8:00–8:40 AM — Results Report

Each student writes a short campaign report (goes in the portfolio):

Goal vs. actual result

Best-performing piece and why

A/B test winner

Cost (money + time) vs. what they got

One thing they would do differently next time

4-5 students share their biggest surprise. Celebrate real results — even small ones. A single real sale from a self-run campaign is a big deal.

#### 8:40–9:00 AM — Week Reflection + Preview Week 25

Reflection: "What did running a real campaign teach you that no lecture could?"

Week 25: Open House Prep. "Next week we get ready to show your business to the community at the Open House. Time to make it real in person."
