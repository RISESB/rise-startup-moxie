# Week 18: Semester 1 Review: Where You Are and Where You Are Going

Unit 2: Customer & Product

**Guiding question:** What have you actually built so far — and what does your business need most in the next 18 weeks?

## By the end of the week, students can
- Audit their business progress across Units 1 and 2.
- Identify the strongest and weakest part of their business right now.
- Set 3 concrete goals for Semester 2.
- Rebuild momentum after the break.

## Lesson walkthrough
_Written as the facilitator's script for the classroom. Use it to know what was taught and how; don't read it back to students._

### Monday

### Monday — Semester 1 Reset + Business Audit

Facilitator Goal: Students come back from break, reconnect to their business, and honestly assess where they are.

#### 7:30–7:40 AM — Warm-Up: Welcome Back

"In one sentence — what happened with your business over break? Did you think about it, work on it, or forget about it? Be honest." Around the room.

Say: "No judgment. Break is break. Today we get the engine running again."

#### 7:40–8:10 AM — Business Audit

Students work through a written audit of everything they built in Units 1 and 2:

Problem + customer: Who do you serve and what problem do you solve? Is it still the same as Week 1?

May-B-P + MVP: What did you build? What did you test? What did you learn?

Customer discovery: How many real customers have you talked to?

Value proposition + price: What is your offer and what do you charge?

Traction: Any sales, pre-orders, or committed customers yet?

#### 8:10–8:50 AM — Strongest / Weakest

Say: "Circle the strongest part of your business — the thing you feel most confident about. Underline the weakest — the thing that scares you or you keep avoiding."

Pairs share both. Partner asks: "Why is that the weak spot? What would it take to fix it?"

Whole group: 4-5 students share their weakest area. Note patterns on the board — these shape where you push this semester.

#### 8:50–9:00 AM — Close

Preview: "Tomorrow you turn this audit into 3 real goals for Semester 2 and start building your business portfolio."

### Tuesday

### Tuesday — Portfolio Build + Goal Setting

#### 7:30–7:35 AM — Warm-Up

"What is the one thing you most want to be true about your business by the end of the year?" Around the room.

#### 7:35–8:10 AM — Business Portfolio

Students assemble everything into one organized place (Google Doc, Slides, or folder): persona, BMC, value proposition, price + rationale, SWOT, sales funnel, pitch deck from Week 17, and any traction data.

Say: "This is your business portfolio. From now on, it is the single source of truth for your business. You will add to it every unit."

#### 8:10–8:45 AM — Semester 2 Goals

Each student writes 3 SMART goals for Semester 2 (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound).

A traction goal — a real number: sales, customers, or revenue

A skill goal — something you want to get better at (marketing, sales, finance)

A growth goal — how you want to grow as an entrepreneur

#### 8:45–9:00 AM — Speaker Tour Prep

Distribute Speaker Tour Prep Worksheets. Prepare for Wednesday.

### Wednesday

### Wednesday — Speaker/Tour

Run the speaker or tour per standard Wednesday format.

### Thursday

### Thursday — Speaker Recap + Thank You Cards + Work Day

#### 7:30–7:35 AM — Warm-Up

"One sentence — most interesting thing from Wednesday." Around the room.

#### 7:35–7:55 AM — Speaker Recap

Two columns: "What we heard" | "How it connects to my Semester 2 goals"

#### 7:55–8:20 AM — Thank You Cards

Handwritten. Specific. Required elements: one specific observation, one question it sparked, a genuine thank you.

#### 8:20–9:00 AM — Portfolio + Goals Work Day

Students finish portfolio and finalize goals. 1-on-1 focus: "What is your #1 traction goal, and what is the first step this week?"

### Friday

### Friday — Profit or Broke + 1-on-1s + Semester 2 Kickoff

#### 7:30–7:35 AM — Warm-Up

"Say your #1 Semester 2 goal out loud." Around the room. Post them somewhere visible all semester.

#### 7:35–8:05 AM — Profit or Broke (Week 18 — Rotation: Profit or Broke)

Present 3-4 scenarios with revenue and cost numbers. Students vote profit or broke, then explain. Use student businesses where possible.

#### 8:05–8:40 AM — 1-on-1 Sessions

1-on-1 focus: "Are your goals realistic and specific? What is the very first action?"

#### 8:40–8:55 AM — Semester 2 Kickoff

Say: "Semester 1 was about building your business. Semester 2 is about growing it — getting customers to find you, and making the numbers work. Unit 3 starts next week with marketing."

#### 8:55–9:00 AM — Preview Week 19

Week 19: Customer Segmentation + Messaging. "Next week: who exactly are you talking to, and what do you say to make them listen?"
