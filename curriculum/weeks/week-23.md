Unit 3: Marketing & Brand

## Lesson walkthrough
_Written as the facilitator's script for the classroom. Use it to know what was taught and how; don't read it back to students._

### Monday

### Monday — Lecture: AI Campaigns + A/B Testing Basics

Facilitator Goal: Students can use AI to build a full campaign quickly and understand how to test versions instead of guessing.

#### 7:30–7:35 AM — Warm-Up

"If you had to double your reach this week with $0, what would you try?" 3-4 students share.

#### 7:35–8:05 AM — Lecture: AI Campaigns + A/B Testing

Opening (2 min)

Say: "Two weeks ago you built one asset. This week you build a whole campaign — and instead of guessing what works, you test it. That is the difference between hoping and knowing."

What Is a Campaign? (6 min)

Say: "A campaign is a set of coordinated marketing pieces with one goal, over a set period of time. Not one random post — a planned push."

A simple campaign has: one goal, one segment, one offer, and 3-5 pieces (posts, emails, a landing page) that all point to the same next step.

Using AI to Build It (8 min)

Say: "AI is fastest here. You give it the strategy — segment, message, brand voice, offer — and it drafts the pieces. You direct and edit."

Example prompt: "I run [business] for [segment]. My brand voice is [3 words]. I want a 1-week campaign to get [goal]. Draft: 3 social captions, 1 short video script, and 1 email — all pointing to [offer/next step]."

"The AI gives you a starting draft in seconds. Your job is to make it true and make it sound like you."

What Is A/B Testing? (10 min)

Say: "A/B testing means showing two versions and letting the data pick the winner. Version A and Version B, changing ONE thing, and you keep whichever performs better."

What you can A/B test:

Headline or hook (the first line)

Image or thumbnail

Call to action ("Buy now" vs. "Try free")

Price framing or offer

The rule: change ONE thing at a time. If you change the image AND the caption, you won't know which one made the difference.

"You do not need fancy tools. Post version A Monday, version B Wednesday, same time. Compare views, clicks, or replies. That is a real test."

#### 8:05–8:50 AM — Campaign Plan

Students plan a 1-week campaign: goal, segment, offer, and the 3-5 pieces. Then they pick ONE element to A/B test and write both versions.

Walk the room: "What is the one thing you are testing? Are A and B different in exactly one way?"

#### 8:50–9:00 AM — Close

Preview: "Tomorrow you build the whole campaign with AI and set up your test."

### Tuesday

### Tuesday — Activities: Build an AI Campaign + Design an A/B Test

#### 7:30–7:35 AM — Warm-Up

"What is your campaign goal and offer in one sentence?" Around the room.

#### 7:35–8:30 AM — Build the Campaign

Students use AI to generate all campaign pieces, then edit each one for truth and voice. Assemble into their portfolio as a "Campaign Kit."

Requirements: all pieces share one offer and point to one next step; brand voice is consistent; the A/B versions are ready.

#### 8:30–8:45 AM — A/B Test Setup

Students write their test plan: "I am testing [element]. Version A is [x], Version B is [y]. I will measure [metric]. I will run it [when]. The winner is the one with more [metric]."

#### 8:45–9:00 AM — Speaker Tour Prep

Distribute Speaker Tour Prep Worksheets.

### Friday

### Friday — Deal or No Deal + 1-on-1s + Week Reflection

#### 7:30–7:35 AM — Warm-Up

"Any early results from your campaign or test?" Around the room.

#### 7:35–8:05 AM — Deal or No Deal (Week 23 — Rotation: Deal or No Deal)

Marketing-decision edition:

Deal 1: "An agency will run all your marketing and AI campaigns for $500/month, guaranteed 1,000 new views a week. Deal or no deal?"

Deal 2: "A tool will auto-generate 30 posts a month for you for $20 — but they all sound a little generic. Deal or no deal?"

Deal 3: "Your A/B test shows version B doubles clicks, but B is off-brand and a little clickbaity. Deal or no deal — do you run it?"

#### 8:05–8:40 AM — 1-on-1 Sessions

1-on-1 focus: "What did your test tell you? What are you keeping and what are you cutting?"

#### 8:40–8:55 AM — Week Reflection

What did building a full campaign with AI teach you?

What did your A/B test reveal?

How does testing change the way you make marketing decisions?
