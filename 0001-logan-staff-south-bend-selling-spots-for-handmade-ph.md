# South Bend selling spots for handmade phone charms

## Brief
A RISE (Startup Moxie) student makes handmade phone charms and wants to sell them in person in South Bend, Indiana. They already know about the RISE school Open House, so don't include that one. Find 5 other real, current places in South Bend where a student could sell handmade phone charms face-to-face — this could include local maker markets, craft fairs, pop-up events, or shops/boutiques that carry items from local makers on consignment or wholesale. For each one, provide: the name, what it is in one or two sentences, and specifically how a student would go about asking to sell there (who to contact, an application process, a typical fee or commission if known, or the general approach). The goal is to help the student pick which one to try first.

## Result (done)
I found 5 real places in South Bend where you could ask to sell your phone charms in person, plus 3 more worth a look. The full table is in the attached file, research.md.

Start with Make South Bend. It's a makerspace that sells local makers' work and also runs holiday markets, so one conversation could open more than one door.

The other four: the South Bend Artisan Market at the Century Center, the Christmas Bonanza Craft Market at St. Hedwig's, the Made With Grace bazaar at Grace United Methodist Church, and the South Bend Farmers Market.

Two things to know before you reach out:
- I could not confirm an open vendor application for any of them. Some fall deadlines have probably passed, so ask about the next market too.
- I could not find table fees, or whether a vendor has to be 18. Ask every one of them, and bring a RISE staff member or a parent in before you sign or pay anything.

The file also has a short script for what to say when you ask, and the links I used.
