# South Bend selling spots for handmade phone charms

## Brief
A RISE (Startup Moxie) student makes handmade phone charms and wants to sell them in person in South Bend, Indiana. They already know about the RISE school Open House, so don't include that one. Find 5 other real, current places in South Bend where a student could sell handmade phone charms face-to-face — this could include local maker markets, craft fairs, pop-up events, or shops/boutiques that carry items from local makers on consignment or wholesale. For each one, provide: the name, what it is in one or two sentences, and specifically how a student would go about asking to sell there (who to contact, an application process, a typical fee or commission if known, or the general approach). The goal is to help the student pick which one to try first.

## Done looks like
A table with 5 real South Bend places, each with name / what it is / how to ask to sell there — specific enough that the student can pick one and reach out this week.
