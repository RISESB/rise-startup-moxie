# Business Snapshot

One page that lets a stranger understand the business in a minute. It's built from evidence the student already has, not from new claims.

## What you need from the student
Ask for these and work with whatever is real. Mark anything missing as "to get" instead of filling it in.
- Business name and one-sentence value proposition
- The problem and who has it
- What they sell and the price
- Proof: customers, sales, interviews, test results, quotes
- The numbers: contribution margin, break-even, revenue so far
- What they learned and changed along the way
- What's next, and what they need

## Steps
1. Pull what they have into the sections below. Their numbers, their words.
2. Find the strongest piece of evidence and put it near the top.
3. Cut anything that's a hope and not a fact, or label it clearly as a goal.
4. Keep the whole thing to one page: about 250 words plus the numbers.

## Output
**[Business name]**: [one-sentence value proposition]

**The problem** (2 sentences)
**Who it's for** (the persona, 1 sentence)
**What we do** (2 sentences, plus price)
**Proof it works** (3 bullets with real numbers or quotes)
**The numbers** (price, contribution margin, break-even, revenue to date)
**What I learned** (the biggest thing they got wrong and fixed)
**What's next** (the next 90 days)
**The ask** (what they want from the reader)

**Still to get before showcase:** a short list.

## Then
Offer to turn it into talking points for the showcase pitch (see the micro-pitch skill).
