# Weekly Loop

Loops beat plans. Each week is one lap: try something real, look at what happened, decide what it means, pick the next test. This is the OODA Loop from Week 1 (observe, orient, decide, act) pointed at the student's own business.

## What you need from the student
- What they actually did for the business this week. Actions, not intentions.
- What happened: numbers if they have them, quotes if they don't.
- Last week's next step, if they remember it.

## Steps
1. **Observe.** List what they did and what happened. Counts beat feelings: messages sent, replies, conversations, sales, dollars.
2. **Orient.** For each result ask: what does this tell us that we didn't know? Sort into: confirmed, surprised, still unknown.
3. **Check last week's promise.** Did they do what they said? If not, what got in the way? No shame, just the real reason, because that's the thing to fix.
4. **Decide.** Pick one of three for each thing they tried: keep doing it, change one thing, or stop.
5. **Act.** One test for next week, with a number that would count as a win and a number that would count as a miss. Small enough to do with what they have.

## Output
**Week of [date]**

| I tried | What happened (numbers) | What it tells me |
|---|---|---|

- **Biggest signal this week:** one sentence.
- **Last week's step:** done / not done, and why.
- **Keep / change / stop:** one line each.
- **Next week's test:** "I will [action, with a quantity] by [day]. Win = [number]. Miss = [number]."
- **One thing to bring to my 1-on-1:** a question or a decision they need help with.

## If they did nothing this week
Don't lecture. Ask what got in the way, shrink the next step until it fits in 20 minutes, and set that as the test.

## Keeping the loop going
Offer to save the review with `request_save` so next week's lap can start from this one.
