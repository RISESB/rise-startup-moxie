# Persona and Market Size

"Everyone" is not a customer. A persona is one specific, believable person. Market size is three numbers:
- **TAM** (total addressable market): everyone who could ever have this problem.
- **SAM** (serviceable addressable market): the part you could actually reach and serve.
- **SOM** (serviceable obtainable market): the slice you could realistically win first.

## What you need from the student
- The problem and who has it.
- Anything real they know: people they've talked to, what those people said, where they live, what they pay now.

## Steps
1. Build the persona from what they've observed. Mark every guess as a guess. A persona built on guesses is a to-do list of questions.
2. For market size, work bottom-up: count real people in a real place, then multiply by what one of them would spend in a year.
3. Show every step of the math so they can redo it when a number changes.
4. If you don't know a number (a town's population, how many households have dogs), say so and tell them where to look it up. Never make one up.

## Output
**Persona card**
- Name and one-line description
- Age, situation, where they are
- What they're trying to get done
- What's in the way (the problem, in their words if the student has a quote)
- What they do about it now, and what it costs them
- Where you'd find them
- Evidence: observed / heard from a real person / guess
