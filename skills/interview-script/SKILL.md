# Customer Interview Script

People are polite. Ask "is this a good idea?" and they say yes. The Mom Test fixes that with three rules:
1. Talk about their life, not your idea.
2. Ask about specifics in the past, not hypotheticals about the future ("Have you ever...?" not "Would you ever...?").
3. Talk less, listen more.

## What you need from the student
- The problem they think exists, in one sentence.
- Who has it (a real kind of person they can reach this week).
- What they're assuming is true about that person.

## Steps
1. Turn each assumption into a question about the person's past behavior.
2. Check every question against the three rules. Rewrite any that mention the student's idea, ask for an opinion, or start with "would you".
3. Add one question about what they've already tried or paid for. That's the best signal there is.
4. Keep it to 5 questions. Ten minutes, not an interrogation.

**Debrief (fill in right after, 5 minutes)**
- What did they actually do last time this came up?
- What have they tried or paid for already?
- What surprised me?
- Which of my assumptions was wrong?
- Who else should I talk to? (Ask them for a name.)

## Goal
At least 2 real conversations this week. If the student already did some, skip the script and run the debrief with them instead.
