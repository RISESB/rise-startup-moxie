# Campaign and A/B Test

A/B testing beats guessing: show two versions that differ in exactly one thing, to similar people, and let the results pick. "Everyone" is not a segment, so the campaign speaks to one group.

## What you need from the student
- The segment: one specific group, and where those people already are (a channel the student can actually use).
- The offer and the one action they want people to take.
- What they can track: views, clicks, replies, sign-ups, sales.

## Steps
1. Write the campaign goal as a number and a date.
2. Pick the 1 or 2 channels that fit the segment. More channels is not better.
3. Draft version A and version B. Change one thing only: the headline, the image, the offer or the call to action.
4. Set the winner rule before launch: which metric, how many people, how long.
5. Lay out the week day by day, including a mid-week check.

## Output
**Goal:** __ [metric] by [date].
**Segment:** ... **Channel(s):** ...
**Message for this segment:** what they care about, in their words.

**A/B test**
| | Version A | Version B |
|---|---|---|
| The one thing that's different | | |
| Full copy | | |

**Winner rule:** "Whichever gets more [metric] after [N people or N days] wins."

**The week**
| Day | Do | Track |
|---|---|---|

**Tracker columns to set up:** date, version, reach, clicks, leads, sales.
**Mid-week check:** what I'll look at and what I'd change.
**After:** what worked, what didn't, what I'd do differently.

## Keep it honest and safe
Only claims that are true. No one else's photos or names without permission. Post from the business or program account, not a personal one, and check with a staff member before spending money on ads.
