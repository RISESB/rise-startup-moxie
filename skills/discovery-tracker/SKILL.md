# Discovery Tracker

Startup Moxie starts with the problem, not the solution. Students find problems by Walk, Talk, Show Up: walking their world looking for friction, gaps and patterns; talking to people about their experience (not pitching); and showing up where the problem lives.

## What you need from the student
- 3 or more things they actually saw, heard or ran into this week. Real ones. If they have none, don't invent any: send them out with the 20-minute assignment at the bottom and stop.
- Where each one happened and who it happened to.

## Steps
1. Write each observation as a tracker entry. Keep their words. Don't upgrade a vague observation into a business idea.
2. Ask which entry bugs them most or shows up most often. That's the one to dig into.
3. Run the 5 Whys on it with them, one why at a time. Let them answer each one. Stop when you hit something a person could actually change.
4. Name what they still don't know, and who could tell them.

## Output
A table they can paste into their tracker:

| # | What I noticed | Where / who | Friction, gap or pattern? | How often? |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Then:
- **Strongest problem:** one sentence, no solution in it.
- **5 Whys:** the chain, numbered.
- **Root cause guess:** one sentence, marked as a guess.
- **Next person to talk to:** a real kind of person they can reach this week.

## If they have nothing yet
Give them this and end there: "Take 20 minutes today. Walk one place you already go (school, work, a store, your block). Write down 3 things that were slow, broken or annoying, and who it annoyed. Bring them back."
