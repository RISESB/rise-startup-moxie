# May-B-P Test Plan

A May-B-P is a business you can build in 2 weeks that tests whether the market is interested in solving the problem, not whether this is the scalable solution. It is not the dream business. It is the first experiment.

It's different from an MVP: an MVP is the smallest version of the product. A May-B-P is the smallest test of whether someone will pay.

## What you need from the student
- The problem, who has it, and their idea for solving it.
- What they already have: skills, tools, people they know, a few dollars. Start with what's in hand.

## Steps
1. **Now vs. Later.** List everything the dream version needs. Sort each item: needed to make the first sale (Now) or not (Later). Be strict. Logos, websites, apps and LLCs are almost always Later.
2. Offer 3 May-B-P versions, from tiny to small. Each must fit in 2 weeks and cost close to nothing.
3. Let the student pick. Then name the riskiest assumption that version tests.
4. Set a pass/fail number before they start, so the result can't be argued with afterward.

## Output
**The dream:** one sentence.
**Now vs. Later**

| Now (needed for sale #1) | Later |
|---|---|

**3 May-B-P options:** for each: what you'd do, what it costs, what it tests.
**The one I'm running:** ...
**Riskiest assumption:** "I believe that ___."
**The test:** what I'll do, with whom, by what date.
**It passes if:** a specific number (e.g. "3 of 10 people pay $5").
**It fails if:** a specific number.
**First step, today, 30 minutes or less:** ...
**Either way I learn:** ...
