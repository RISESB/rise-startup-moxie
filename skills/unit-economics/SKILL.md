# Unit Economics and Break-Even

- **Revenue** is what the customer pays. **Profit** is what's left after costs. They are not the same.
- **Contribution margin** = price of one sale minus the costs that come with that one sale.
- **Break-even** = fixed costs divided by contribution margin. It's how many sales it takes before you stop losing money.
- Profit on paper is not cash in hand. When money comes in and goes out matters.

## What you need from the student
- Price of one sale.
- Costs per sale: materials, packaging, fees, and their own time (use an hourly number they choose).
- Fixed costs per month: things they pay whether they sell anything or not.
- Real numbers. If they don't know one, flag it and use a placeholder they must replace. Never invent a cost.

## Steps
1. Build the one-sale table.
2. Calculate contribution margin in dollars and as a percent of price.
3. Calculate break-even in units and in dollars.
4. Show a simple 3-scenario month: slow, expected, good.
5. Name the one number that matters most. Usually it's the one that changes break-even the fastest.
